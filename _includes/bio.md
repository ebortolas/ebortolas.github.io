My background is in Physics and Astrophysics. I started my scientific education at the <b>University of Milano-Bicocca, in Italy</b>, where I got both my bachelor degree in Physics (2013)
and my master degree in Astrophysics and Space Physics (2015) under the supervision of Massimo Dotti and Alessia Gualandris, working on the dynamics of supermassive binaries formed as a result of a galaxy merger.
In October 2015 I started my PhD at the INAF-Osservatorio Astronomico di Padova under the supervision of Michela Mapelli, working on the dynamics of stars in galactic nuclei.
During both my master and PhD, I spent a total of 16 months at the University of Surrey (UK), where I worked with Alessia Gualandris and Justin Read, investigating the properties of the stellar systems hosting a supermassive black hole binary.
After my PhD, I spent 2 years as a postdoctoral researcher at the Institute for Computational Science of the University of Zurich, working in the group led by Lucio Mayer. There, I mainly worked on the large scale dynamics of massive black hole pairs in the cosmological context; I also studied the dynamics of extreme mass ratio inspirals.
In November 2020 I moved back to Milan, where I got a Postdoc position in the B Massive group led by Alberto Sesana at the University of Milano Bicocca. In this timeframe, I also got very interested in the dynamics of tidal disruption events.
The study of these sources gave me the idea for a project through which I have been awarded a Marie Curie Global Postdoctoral Fellowship in 2022, that I decided to spend at the University of Milano Bicocca and at Princeton University.
Since October 2024 I am a researcher at the INAF-Osservatorio Astronomico di Padova, where I recently (2026) got a permanent researcher position. My most recent interests expanded towards the scientific applications of the instrument MORFEO (ELT), as well as the development of L-Galaxies, a semi-analytical model for the evolution of galaxies and massive black holes.
