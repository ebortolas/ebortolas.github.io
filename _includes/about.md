I am a permanent researcher in astrophysics at the [INAF-Osservatorio Astronomico di Padova](https://www.oapd.inaf.it/)
within the [Italian National Institute for Astrophysics](http://www.inaf.it/en?set_language=en).
My research focuses on the <b>physics of galactic nuclei</b>, where my favourite astrophysical objects, (super)massive black holes, reside.
I spend most of my time modelling the <b>dynamics of stars and gas about single or binary massive black holes</b> through numerical simulations and theoretical studies.
I am especially interested in the study of massive black hole binaries, stellar tidal disruption events, extreme-mass-ratio inspirals. In the bigger picture, my research aims at better understanding the sources targeted by present and future gravitational wave detectors (<b>LISA</b>, Einstein Telescope, the European Pulsar Timing Array) and electromagnetic facilities (such as <b>MORFEO@ELT</b>, as well as transient surveys as LSST-Vera Rubin).
